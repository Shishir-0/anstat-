### Q: What services does ANSTAT provide?

### Q: Can we engage ANSTAT for a single division, or must we hire all four?
**A:** You can engage ANSTAT for a specific standalone service—such as building a custom web application, producing commercial videography, running performance ad campaigns, or deploying an AI agent—or as an integrated partner managing both engineering and commercial growth.

### Q: How should a business evaluate a digital marketing agency in Bengaluru?
**A:** Evaluate agencies on three empirical criteria: First, verifiable commercial outcomes (cost-per-lead, conversion rates, pipeline revenue). Second, full-funnel execution capability (in-house video production, copywriting, landing page engineering). Third, clean attribution tracking so you know exactly which campaigns generated qualified sales inquiries.

### Q: What is the difference between SEO and paid performance advertising?
**A:** Paid advertising (Meta Ads, Google Search, LinkedIn) delivers immediate pipeline velocity and targeted volume, but traffic ceases once ad spend stops. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) builds compounding organic discoverability and domain authority over time. We typically recommend a hybrid approach: performance ads for immediate pipeline, SEO for sustainable long-term acquisition.
