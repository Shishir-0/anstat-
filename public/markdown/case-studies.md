# ANSTAT Case Studies & Portfolio Evidence

> Documented commercial client engagements and internal engineering demonstrations delivered by ANSTAT across custom software development, applied AI, commercial videography, and growth marketing.

## 1. UK GradLink Admissions Platform
- **Client**: UK GradLink
- **Industry**: International Higher Education & Admissions
- **Challenge**: Manual document verification, fragmented applicant emails, and slow processing times creating applicant drop-off during peak intake cycles.
- **Solution**: Developed a centralized admissions platform with role-based access, document processing, and real-time applicant status tracking.
- **Technologies**: React, Vite, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS
- **Outcome**: Eliminated manual verification bottlenecks and provided international applicants with real-time application transparency.

## 2. LVBL Digital Commerce & Infrastructure
- **Client**: LVBL
- **Industry**: Lifestyle & Digital Commerce
- **Challenge**: Disconnected product catalogs, high customer acquisition costs, and poor mobile checkout conversion rates.
- **Solution**: Engineered a responsive storefront, centralized inventory synchronization, and configured multi-channel acquisition funnels.
- **Technologies**: React, Node.js, Commerce APIs, Meta Ads, GA4
- **Outcome**: Streamlined product purchasing flow and delivered measurable improvements in customer acquisition efficiency.

## 3. Universal PU College Ramanagara
- **Client**: Universal PU College
- **Domain**: upuc.in
- **Industry**: Higher Secondary Education

- **Technologies**: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Institutional Lead Routing
