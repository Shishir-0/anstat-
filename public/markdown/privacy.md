# Privacy Policy | ANSTAT

> Effective Date: October 11, 2026. Last Updated: November 7, 2026.

## 1. Overview
ANSTAT ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, operates the website https://anstat.co.in. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding the collection, handling, and protection of information when you interact with our website, services, and engineering desk.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information only to facilitate business inquiries, deliver agreed commercial services, and ensure security:
- **Contact Details**: Name, corporate email address, telephone number, company name, and project scope submitted through our consultation forms or direct communications to connect@anstat.co.in.
- **Technical & Telemetry Data**: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic data including browser type, operating system, approximate geographic region (city level), and anonymized interaction paths via privacy-configured Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager.
- **Client Project Data**: Proprietary datasets, documentation, or credentials provided during active client engagements under executed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

## 3. Strict Data Isolation & Zero AI Training Policy
We enforce strict data isolation protocols:
- **No Third-Party AI Training**: Client proprietary data, source code, and confidential documentation are never shared with or used to train public commercial AI models (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google foundation models).
- **No Selling or Renting**: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or corporate data to data brokers, ad networks, or third parties under any circumstances.

## 4. Analytics & Cookie Governance
Our website uses minimal, functional, and performance analytics cookies:
- All telemetry transmitted via Google Tag Manager and GA4 is sanitized to prevent Personally Identifiable Information (PII) leakage.
- Self-hosted variable fonts and assets are served with zero third-party font tracking.

## 5. Data Retention & Security
- We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest) for all stored records.
- Client engagement data is retained only for the duration required to support SLA obligations and legal compliance, after which it is securely expunged.

## 6. Your Rights
Under applicable Indian Information Technology laws and digital personal data protection principles, you have the right to inspect, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold.
