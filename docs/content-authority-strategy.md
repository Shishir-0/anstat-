## 3. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Citability Guidelines

Following empirical findings from the Princeton GEO study (KDD 2024) and recent AI citation research:

1. **Lead with 40–60 Word Definitive Statements**: Never bury definitions behind narrative fluff. Place the answer immediately beneath the H1 or H2.
2. **Optimal Citability Passage Length (134–167 words)**: Format supporting paragraphs into self-contained units that can be cited in isolation without losing context.
3. **Include Specific Verifiable Metrics**:
   - Sub-500ms voice AI latency
   - 100% intellectual property ownership
   - 24-business-hour inquiry turnaround
   - Zero public model training leakage
4. **Structured Comparison Tables**: Comparison tables receive 3x higher citation frequency than generic prose for "vs" and "best" queries.
5. **Freshness Signals**: Maintain explicit `datePublished` and `dateModified` in JSON-LD and page metadata.

## 4. Internal Linking & Crawl Hierarchy Strategy

Every indexable page follows a strict bidirectional linking topology:
- **Homepage (`/`)** links directly to:
  - All 5 dedicated landing pages (`/marketing-agency-bengaluru`, `/media-production`, `/business-solutions`, `/ai-solutions`, `/software-development`)
  - Broad directory (`/services`)
  - Evidence hub (`/case-studies`)
  - R&D blueprint (`/research/ai-startup-architecture`)
  - Trust pages (`/about`, `/faq`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`)
- **Service Landing Pages** link directly to:
  - Relevant case studies demonstrating verified results
  - Related sibling services (e.g. Marketing Agency links to Media Production and Software Development)
  - Dedicated `#enquiry` and `/contact` conversion endpoints
- **Case Studies (`/case-studies`)** link directly to:
  - Corresponding service divisions that engineered the solution
  - Client domains (`upuc.in`, `duskenterprise.com`) as third-party evidence
- **Footer Navigation** uniformly exposes discoverable links to all 14 routes on every single URL of the domain.
